## Who we are

CupQ is a coffee ordering service for events, run by [legal name, ABN,
address]. You can reach us at hello@cupq.com.au.

When you order a coffee at an event, the event's organiser has booked CupQ.
[The organiser decides why your details are collected; CupQ handles them on
the organiser's behalf. Lawyer to confirm the controller/processor split.]

## What we collect when you order a coffee

- **Your first name** (or the name you type), so the barista can call it
  and the screen can show it when your coffee is ready.
- **Your mobile number, if you give it.** It's optional. We use it to text
  you when your coffee is ready and to help you find your order. If you
  order by text, we have the number you texted from.
- **Your order:** the drink, milk, size, any notes, the time and the coffee
  station.
- **Things you choose to save,** such as your usual drink. If you type an
  allergy into a note, we keep it with the order so the barista sees it.
  [Lawyer: disclaimer that CupQ is not a food-allergy service.]
- **If the event uses EventsAir,** the organiser may link their attendee
  list (your name, email, mobile, badge number and registration category)
  so you're recognised when you order.

We don't collect payment card details. If an event charges for coffee, the
payment is handled by Square under their privacy policy.

## On your phone

The ordering page remembers your current order in your phone's browser so
you can come back to it. Nothing is tracked across other websites, and we
don't use advertising cookies.

## How we use it

Only to make and hand over your coffee: show your name on the board, text
you when it's ready, let you check its status, and give the organiser a
report of how many coffees were made (counts and times, not names).

We don't sell your details, use them for advertising, or send you marketing.
The text when your coffee is ready is part of the order you placed.
[Lawyer: Spam Act; whether "reply STOP" wording is needed.]

## Who else handles it

To run the service we use:

- **Railway** (servers and databases, USA and Singapore)
- **Cloudflare** (web security, global)
- **Twilio** (text messages, USA)
- and, only if the event uses them, **EventsAir** (attendee list) and
  **Square** (payments).

This means your details are stored and processed **outside Australia**,
mainly in the USA and Singapore. [APP 8 wording for the lawyer.]

The event organiser and their baristas can see the names and orders for
their event while it runs.

## How long we keep it

When the event is over, the organiser retires it. That erases names, mobile
numbers, notes, text conversations and the attendee list, and keeps only
the anonymous counts for the report. Organisers can also set details to be
erased automatically a number of days after each order.

Backups taken before the erase are kept for [14] days and then deleted.

## Keeping it safe

Each event gets its own copy of CupQ. Pages anyone can open show first
names and part of a phone number at most. Everything else needs a staff
login, and admin logins can require a code from a phone. Connections are
encrypted (HTTPS).

## Your choices

- You can order without giving a mobile number.
- You can ask us what we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it:
  hello@cupq.com.au. If it's during an event, the organiser can do it
  straight away.
- If you're unhappy with how we've handled your details, tell us first. If
  we don't sort it out, you can contact the Office of the Australian
  Information Commissioner (oaic.gov.au).
