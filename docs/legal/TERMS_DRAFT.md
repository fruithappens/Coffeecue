# CupQ terms of service for clients (draft)

**DRAFT for legal review, 15 Oct 2026. Not in force. Not legal advice.**
Roadmap L6-1. These are the terms between CupQ and the organisation that
books it (the "client"). Attendees see the privacy policy, not these.
Text in [square brackets] is for CupQ to fill in or the lawyer to decide.

---

## 1. The service

CupQ provides coffee ordering for the client's event: ordering by phone,
kiosk and text, barista screens, the pickup board, cup labels if hardware
is hired, and a report afterwards. The plan (Skim, Flat White, Double Shot
or The Urn) and any hardware are as set out in the quote.

## 2. The client's part

- Give CupQ accurate event details: dates, times, expected numbers,
  stations, menu.
- Provide what the venue needs to supply: power, and internet if the plan
  relies on it (most phones use their own mobile data).
- Make sure the client has the right to share any attendee list it links
  (for example from EventsAir), and that attendees have been told their
  details may be used to serve their coffee.
- Keep staff logins private and remove people who no longer need access.

## 3. Attendee data

- The client decides what attendee data the event uses. CupQ handles it
  only to run the service, under the privacy policy.
- After the event, attendee details are erased when the event is retired
  or after the number of days the client chooses. Anonymous counts are
  kept for the report.
- Data is stored outside Australia (USA, Singapore). An Australian region
  is available on request [for an additional fee / from date].
- CupQ will tell the client without undue delay if it becomes aware of a
  data breach affecting the client's attendees. [Lawyer: NDB scheme
  wording.]

## 4. Text messages

- Included texts are as set out in the plan. Texts beyond the allowance are
  charged at [rate] per text. One text can count as two or more if it is
  long or uses special characters; the Runner's text meter shows the count.
- Texts are sent through Twilio. CupQ is not responsible for delays or
  failures in mobile networks. If texts stop working, the board and each
  customer's phone page still show their order.

## 5. Hardware (if hired)

The client looks after hired hardware during the event and returns it in
the same condition, allowing for fair wear. Loss or damage is charged at
replacement cost [as listed in the kit list].

## 6. Fees and payment

Fees are as quoted, plus GST. [Deposit, payment terms, cancellation
schedule.]

## 7. Availability and support

CupQ aims to keep the service running throughout the event and monitors it
during service. Support during the event is as set out in the plan [email /
event-day email / event-day phone]. Planned updates are never made while an
event is live.

## 8. Liability

- CupQ is not responsible for the coffee itself, its preparation or
  ingredients, or for allergy or dietary information typed into an order.
  That is a matter for the barista service.

## 9. Ending the service

Either party may end the agreement for a serious breach not fixed within
[14] days of notice. At the end, the client can download its event report.
CupQ then erases attendee details and may stop the client's copy.
